# popular-science research

Fetched 2026-09-17. Reference = model knows it; open source only if unsure. Full entry = model did not know it; cite the quote.

## Jargon
- [digital.gov avoid jargon](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/principles/avoid-jargon) - keep needed technical terms, define each; readers' top complaint

## Story vs list
- [NN/g bulleted lists](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/presenting-bulleted-lists/) - vertical list for 3+ parallel items; page of dots daunts
- [Dahlstrom PNAS 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4183170/): "Narrative text was read twice as fast and recalled twice as well, regardless of topic familiarity or interest in the content itself". Rule: argument as narrative, not bullets. Tell: causal story as bullets.

## Lede
- [NN/g inverted pyramid](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/inverted-pyramid/) - conclusion first; reader can stop anywhere and keep the point

## Scanning, paragraphs
- [NN/g how little users read 2008](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-little-do-users-read/) - average visit reads ~20% of words, 28% at most
- [digital.gov short paragraphs](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/writing/clear-short): "Writing experts recommend paragraphs of no more than 150 words in three to eight sentences." Rule: one topic per paragraph; never over 250 words. Tell: block past 150 words.

## Translation exaggeration
- [Sumner BMJ 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4262123/) - releases exaggerate advice 40%, causal 33%, animal→human 36%; news copies them

## Sourcing, magnitude
- [SMC reporting guidelines](https://www.sciencemediacentre.co.nz/coveringscience/best-practice-guidelines-for-reporting-on-science/) - state source, size, stage; absolute risk; causal vs link; limits
- [Gigerenzer PSPI 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2008.00033.x) - absolute risks, natural frequencies; relative risk misleads

## Ending
- [Poynter endings 2016](https://www.poynter.org/educators-students/2016/want-a-strong-ending-to-your-story-here-are-3-tips/) - plant kicker high, circle back; twist or closing quote

## Emphasis
- [NN/g how users read 1997](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-users-read-on-the-web/) - 79% scan; highlighted keywords, one idea per paragraph, half the words

## Visuals
- [Franconeri PSPI 2021](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/visual-data-communication.html): "Understand how starting axes at zero might not always be the best option because it can mask relevant data patterns or create the illusion of patterns that do not reflect reality." Rule: legends → data labels; axis span by meaningful effect, not a zero rule. Tell: zero baseline demanded on reflex.
- [Correll 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02035) - truncation exaggerates effect even with explicit break cues
- [Okabe, Ito colour](https://jfly.uni-koeln.de/color/) - 8% of men red-green blind; pair colour with shape, line, pattern

Unsourced: names in context; concreteness; condescension; false vagueness.
