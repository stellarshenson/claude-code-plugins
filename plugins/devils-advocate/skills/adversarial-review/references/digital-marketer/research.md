# digital-marketer research

Fetched 2026-09-23. Reference = model knows it; open source only if unsure. Full entry = model did not know it; cite the quote.

## Message, concreteness
- [NN/g homepage](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/113-design-guidelines-homepage-usability/) - one glance: where users are, what company does, what they can do
- [NN/g concise, objective 1997](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/) - objective not promotional +27%; all three changes +124%
- [Unbounce attention ratio](https://unbounce.com/conversion-centered-design/) - more links on a landing page, lower conversion

## Calls to action
- [NN/g Get Started 2024](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/get-started/) - "Get Started" fits any goal; label names the outcome
- [Conversion Rate Experts](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/fantokens-ctas-win-report/) - one test: explicit "Buy" label, +57% conversions

## AI-sounding copy
- [Equilibrium 2025](https://economic-policy.pl/index.php/eq/article/view/4038): "Advertisements labelled as human-generated received higher consumer trust and purchase intent ratings, even if they were AI-generated." Rule: perceived AI authorship lowers trust, most on high-involvement buys. Tell: machine-sounding copy.
- [WSU, Cicek 2024](https://news.wsu.edu/press-release/2024/07/30/using-the-term-artificial-intelligence-in-product-descriptions-reduces-purchase-intentions/): "When AI is mentioned, it tends to lower emotional trust, which in turn decreases purchase intentions." Rule: "AI" as the benefit costs trust. Tell: "AI-powered" headline.
- [Jakesch PNAS 2023](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2023/03/ai-or-human-written-language-assumptions-mislead) - readers detect AI text at chance, 50-52%; judge phrasing, not authorship

## Claims, consumer law
- [FTC substantiation 1984](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation) - objective claims need a reasonable basis before dissemination
- [FTC fake reviews rule 2024](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials) - bans fake, bought, undisclosed-insider reviews, review suppression
- [FTC dark patterns 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/09/ftc-report-shows-rise-sophisticated-dark-patterns-designed-trick-trap-consumers) - fake countdown timers, hidden cancellation deceptive

## Channels
- [Google snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet): "There's no limit on how long a meta description can be, but the snippet is truncated in Google Search results as needed, typically to fit the device width." Rule: no Google length; point first. Tell: a 150-160 character rule.
- [Gmail sender rules](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126) - over 5,000 messages a day: one-click unsubscribe, visible body link
