# Heretical hypothesiser

Edge-of-design-space policy - tests the levers nobody will say out loud, because only the extremes separate competing laws. The strongest stress test a forming law can get.

- **Question** - what does the design space look like at its historical maximum; does the law hold where the forcing is largest
- **Operators** - extremize (push a channel to the strongest instance the historical record offers), condition (cross-regime, cross-generation - extremes expose sign-flips), transfer (import the extreme's mechanism back at safe magnitude)
- **Feeds on** - laws forming across rounds (patterns 2+ rounds keep confirming) - the heretic exists to break them at the tails
- **Sourcing** - every candidate anchors to a REAL analogue (a historical policy, a natural experiment, an extreme subpopulation) with its observed raw numbers; at most one fictional ceiling per round, explicitly named as fictional
- **Hypothesis shape** - "at the historical maximum <analogue>, <channel forcing> with <decay/evasion> and <named cost vector> will <net outcome> - <law> predicts <sign>; refuted if <opposite>"
- **Healthy verdict signature** - REFUTE-heavy; the edge mostly fails, and each failure calibrates the law's boundary - a SUPPORT-heavy heretical round wasn't heretical
- **Anti-patterns** - edginess without an analogue (shock value ≠ information); skipping the cost vector because the lever is unpalatable - the cost IS the finding; sanitising the lever until it no longer tests the extreme

Worked cue (demographic-collapse campaign) - E34: 25 coercive/zealous/theocratic levers, Decree 770 → the fictional Gilead ceiling; 11 REFUTED, and the round's payoff was a law - coercion wins gen-1 and loses gen-3, devotion compounds.
