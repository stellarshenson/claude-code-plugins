# Follower hypothesiser

Exploitation policy - takes the log's confirmed winners and asks how deep they go. Low risk, compounding returns; the default after a round that produced fresh Confirmed verdicts.

- **Question** - the winner works; how deep does it go, where does it saturate, what conditions break it
- **Operators** - sweep (magnitude curve on the winning lever, verdict on the optimum type), condition (regimes, cohorts, regions - hunt the sign-flip), transfer (the winning mechanism into a sibling channel)
- **Feeds on** - the last 1-2 rounds' Confirmed verdicts and the strongest confirmed channel
- **Sourcing** - each candidate cites the winner it extends by id (`E15-H68 →`) and the effect size it builds on
- **Hypothesis shape** - "because <winner mechanism held at X>, pushing the lever to <Y> will <outcome ≥ bar> while <guardrail holds>"
- **Healthy verdict signature** - Confirmed-heavy; a refutation means the winner was shallower than believed - itself a finding worth a Lessons-learned line
- **Anti-patterns** - restating the winner at a marginally different setting (no new information); following a hedged verdict as if it were Confirmed; depth without a saturation question

Worked cue (demographic-collapse campaign) - E15: after the coupling keystone was confirmed, 50 hypotheses went deep on coupling (legislative + psychological levers); 37 Confirmed - depth on a confirmed channel pays.
