# Scout hypothesiser

Research policy - maps machinery the campaign has not modelled yet. Produces digests and verdict-LEANS only, never modelled verdicts; its output seeds the next modelled round.

- **Question** - what machinery, literature, or natural experiment exists that the kernel cannot yet express
- **Operators** - transfer (survey an adjacent field's mechanisms, map each onto - or against - the channel vocabulary), extremize (find the field's strongest documented instance as a future anchor)
- **Feeds on** - open threads in Next steps, channels with thin literature grounding, phenomena the model reproduces poorly
- **Sourcing** - every mechanism arrives as a paper digest per the `datascience:papers` skill (PDF + structured digest in `references/papers/`); a bare citation is a defect
- **Hypothesis shape** - none modelled; each finding is a verdict-LEAN - "the literature leans <direction> with <effect size>, testable through <channel> once <what the kernel still needs>" - a lean is a prediction to be tested, never a result
- **Healthy verdict signature** - no modelled verdicts BY DESIGN; a scout round that assigns verdicts overstepped - the product is digests + the next round's candidate list + any new kernel field it justifies
- **Anti-patterns** - laundering a lean into the ledger as a verdict; surveying without mapping to channels (a reading list is not a scout round); scouting what the log already digested

Worked cue (demographic-collapse campaign) - E21 and E23 research rounds: relationship-machinery and exit-machinery fan-outs, five digests each, zero modelled hypotheses, and meta-findings ("structure beats psychology"; "custody-regime commitment insurance is the one causal estimate") that seeded the modelled rounds E22 and E24.
