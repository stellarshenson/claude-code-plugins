# Article structure templates

Named structures the field uses. Pick one before drafting - most writing problems are structure problems. Each lists its beats in order and when to reach for it.

## Wall Street Journal formula (the feature workhorse)
- **Beats** - anecdotal lead (a specific scene or person) → nut graf (why it matters) → body (facts, quotes, evidence in themed sections) → kicker that circles back to the opening character or scene
- **Use when** - a feature or character-driven story for a general audience; the default for turning one result into a human story
- Source - [Ohio State / feature organization](https://ohiostate.pressbooks.pub/stratcommwriting/chapter/feature-article-organization/)

## Inverted pyramid (news)
- **Beats** - most critical facts first (the five Ws) → supporting detail in descending importance → background last
- **Use when** - a spot announcement or result where the reader may stop at any point and must still get the news; NOT for narrative features
- Source - [Purdue OWL](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/journalism_and_journalistic_writing/the_inverted_pyramid.html)

## Ladder of abstraction / narrative arc
- **Beats** - open on a concrete sensory detail → climb through explanatory rungs → reach the single abstract meaning (the "so what") → close on a visceral moment that carries the meaning back to the concrete
- **Use when** - a deeply reported feature or essay meant to change how the reader sees something
- Source - [The Open Notebook](https://www.theopennotebook.com/2023/05/30/using-the-ladder-of-abstraction-to-elevate-science-stories/)

## Braided / woven narrative
- **Beats** - alternate narrative sections (scene, anecdote) with brief explanatory sections (background, mechanism), threaded throughout rather than staged in one block; hold a one-word theme as the throughline
- **Use when** - long-form with complex background that cannot pause the momentum, or multiple threads to interleave
- Source - [Nieman Storyboard](https://niemanstoryboard.org/2022/02/02/sticking-a-story-together-and-nailing-the-structure/)

## Martini glass (narrative data viz)
- **Beats** - narrow stem: an author-driven opening that poses the question and guides the reader through the key observations → widening mouth: hand off to reader-driven exploration / interactivity
- **Use when** - an explainer built around data or an interactive; guide first, then let the reader roam
- Source - [Segel & Heer, "Narrative Visualization"](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~stasko/7450/Papers/segel-tvcg10.pdf)
