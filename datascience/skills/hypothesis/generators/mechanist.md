# Mechanist hypothesiser

Decomposition policy - takes a winning effect and asks what actually drives it. Splits a confirmed lever into its undercurrents and tests which component is load-bearing.

- **Question** - what undercurrent drives the winning effect; is the named lever the cause or merely the vehicle
- **Operators** - transfer (isolate each candidate component in its own channel), invert (remove the suspected driver - does the effect survive), condition (regimes where the components separate)
- **Feeds on** - strong SUPPORTED verdicts whose Mechanism bullet is thinner than their effect size - big number, hand-wavy why
- **Sourcing** - each decomposition names 2-4 candidate components, each with a literature effect size or an observed signal
- **Hypothesis shape** - "the <winner> effect decomposes into <components>; because <component X> carries <mechanism>, isolating X retains ≥ <share>% of the effect while <the vehicle alone> retains < <floor>%"
- **Healthy verdict signature** - REFRAMED-heavy; the valuable outcome is "the effect is real but the mechanism is different" - zero reframes means the round picked winners already well-understood, or tested too gently
- **Anti-patterns** - decomposing into components the system cannot measure separately; mechanism stories with no discriminating test between them; treating a REFRAMED verdict as failure - it is the finding

Worked cue (demographic-collapse campaign) - E33: religion decomposed into the channels it loads (norm, community, security); establishment religion is inert under high security - the norm channel is the active ingredient, the vehicle was not the cause.
