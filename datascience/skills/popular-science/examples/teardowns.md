# Exemplar teardowns - what best-in-class looks like

Five freely-readable articles from top outlets, torn down on the spine this skill writes to: hook → nut graf → structure → sourcing → imagery → kicker. Study the moves; mirror the shape, not the topic. Quotes are short excerpts from the source; follow the link to read the whole piece.

## 1. The AI Revolution in Math Has Arrived - Quanta, Steven Strogatz, 2026
<https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-ai-revolution-in-math-has-arrived-20260413/>
- **Hook** - a dated turning point: "The tipping point came in the summer of 2025..." (AI models solving five of six IMO problems)
- **Nut graf** - AI now discovers and proves new theorems; the "this is real, now" register
- **Structure** - breakthrough → history (AlphaEvolve) → people → specific results (Bruhat intervals) → cultural tension
- **Sourcing** - named, affiliated, and quoted: Tao (UCLA) - "This guy's got a shovel. This guy's got a pickax"; Venkatesh (IAS); Ellenberg (Wisconsin); both enablers and skeptics
- **Imagery** - permutation-group graphs, Bruhat structures, researcher portraits
- **Kicker** - grounds the excitement in the stakes of losing math's aesthetic dimension; arcs back to the breathless opening
- **Why it works** - names lend authority; balances cheerleading with skepticism; structure mirrors breakthrough→history→people→results→implications

## 2. The Mathematical Mysteries of Fireflies - Nautilus, Chris Baraniuk, 2026
<https://nautil.us/the-mathematical-mysteries-of-fireflies-1204513>
- **Hook** - sensory wonder: "a constellation of hundreds of fireflies blinking in unison"
- **Nut graf** - synchronized flashing reveals oscillation math spanning neurons to superconductors
- **Structure** - wonder → historical skepticism (the Bucks) → researcher biography (Peleg) → field method + density thresholds → chimera states → conservation stakes
- **Sourcing** - Peleg (CU Boulder), Strogatz (Cornell), Ermentrout (Pittsburgh), Lewis (Tufts); grounded in 2021-22 publications and 3D reconstructions
- **Imagery** - prose only, but the prose carries visual weight
- **Kicker** - wonder and science do not mean detachment from environmental stakes; arcs back to the opening image
- **Why it works** - opens on wonder, sustains it through technical depth; researcher biography humanizes abstract math

## 3. The world's deadliest animals - Our World in Data, Ritchie & Roser, 2024
<https://ourworldindata.org/deadliest-animals>
- **Hook** - a shocking number: "One and a half million people are killed by animals every year"
- **Nut graf** - mosquitoes and snakes kill far more than culture suggests, and most deaths are preventable
- **Structure** - mortality stats → surprising ranking reversals → exposure-vs-danger frame → prevention per killer → how to cut deaths
- **Sourcing** - IHME, WHO, specialist datasets, with method docs; ~760,000 mosquito deaths/year; snake deaths cuttable two-thirds with antivenom
- **Imagery** - treemap + bar chart of deaths by animal; the visual does the argument's heavy lifting
- **Kicker** - agency, not fatalism: eliminating mosquito-borne disease could cut deaths nearly sixfold; arcs back to the opening scale
- **Why it works** - shocking stat then systematic deconstruction; data-viz makes abstract numbers concrete; closes on what can be done

## 4. In our deep oceans, evolution is supercharged - The Conversation, Thomas Mock, 2025
<https://theconversation.com/in-our-deep-oceans-evolution-is-supercharged-this-diversity-of-life-could-help-unlock-humanitys-greatest-challenges-286818>
- **Hook** - scale + mystery: "the largest and least explored habitat on Earth"
- **Nut graf** - extreme conditions accelerate evolution, yielding enzymes with biotech uses
- **Structure** - unexplored framing → vent ecosystems + method → mechanism of fast evolution → enzyme discoveries + applications → collaboration + future
- **Sourcing** - Mock (UEA), with the Institute of Oceanology + BGI; ~2,000 samples from HOV Fendouzhe; 500M+ genes, 60% novel proteins, a helicase twice as fast
- **Imagery** - prose-driven, emphasizing submersible data and sequence scale
- **Kicker** - reframes "least explored" as "most valuable"; a wonder→utility turn
- **Why it works** - researcher-as-author translation; concrete numbers ground the "supercharged" claim; scientific and practical stakes

## 5. For Algorithms, a Little Memory Outweighs a Lot of Time - Quanta, Kevin Hartnett, 2025
<https://www.quantamagazine.org/for-algorithms-a-little-memory-outweighs-a-lot-of-time-20250521/>
- **Hook** - stakes + surprise: "a 'stunning' proof is the first progress in 50 years on one of the most famous questions in computer science"
- **Nut graf** - Williams proved memory more powerful than believed; first major time-vs-space breakthrough in 50 years
- **Structure** - the discovery → biography (rural Alabama → MIT) → history (Hartmanis, 1960s) → the path → the "squishy pebbles" technique → P-vs-PSPACE implications
- **Sourcing** - Williams (MIT), Wigderson (IAS), Valiant (Harvard), Aaronson (UT Austin); historical Hartmanis (Cornell)
- **Imagery** - photographs (Williams' office, the researchers); no technical diagrams
- **Kicker** - Williams' own line: "the thing I prove is way better than what I wanted" - serendipity + ambition; echoes the opening's breakthrough-as-gift frame
- **Why it works** - biography makes complexity theory emotionally real; a 50-year stuck-history raises stakes; the closing quote admits uncertainty while celebrating surprise
